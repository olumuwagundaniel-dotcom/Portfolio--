01 / About

More than one lane.

I build with curiosity and purpose.

My journey sits at an interesting intersection: engineering, technology and education. I'm studying Civil Engineering at the University of Lagos while developing practical skills in frontend development and software building. I'm also the Co-Founder of Erudite Elite Tutors, where education and student support are central to the work.

I enjoy turning ideas into digital experiences that are simple to use, visually clear and genuinely useful. But technology is only one side of my story. I also care deeply about learning and helping other students grow.
